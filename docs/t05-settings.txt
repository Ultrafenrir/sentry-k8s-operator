T05 — FILTERS AND PII SETTINGS
Updated: 2026-10-31
Status: Complete for the published alpha surface with live HTTP round-trip evidence

Published settings are limited to:

- inbound filters: browser extensions, localhost, and web crawlers;
- PII: scrub data, scrub defaults, scrub IP addresses, sensitive fields, and safe fields.

Omitted settings remain unmanaged. Present settings are normalized, diffed against the
live project, updated through the narrow public endpoints, and read back. Reconciliation
does not overwrite unrelated project fields and becomes mutation-free after convergence.

Settings mutation requires managed project ownership. A read-only existing-project binding
cannot modify settings. Component conditions separate settings readiness from project/DSN,
destination, and alert readiness so one failure does not erase useful progress.

Sanitized fixtures cover both supported Sentry versions. Disposable live acceptance has
updated and read back all published fields, introduced external drift, required one
controller reconcile to repair it, and confirmed a following no-op reconcile.

Grouping profiles and arbitrary raw settings remain outside v1alpha1 because their portable
semantics are not proven.

Verification: `make verify-t05` plus the configuration portion of
`make sentry-live-config-api` on the disposable target.
